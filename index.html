About Me

I am a Machine Learning Faculty at Creative IT Institute, Bangladesh, with a background in Computer Science & Engineering and research experience across Natural Language Processing, Computer Vision, Social Media Analysis, Sentiment Analysis, Image Captioning, and Recommender Systems.

My research interests lie at the intersection of Machine Learning, Trustworthy AI, Data-Centric AI, and Reproducible AI. I am particularly interested in building robust, reliable, generalizable, and human-centered AI systems for real-world applications.
